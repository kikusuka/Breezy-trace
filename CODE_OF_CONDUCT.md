# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Breezy Trace is a project for developers of all backgrounds and experience levels. We want participation to be welcoming, respectful, and free of harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give actionable, specific feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information, credentials, tokens, or repository data.
- Keep discussions on topic and avoid spam or repeated promotion.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, and ask for clarification when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository profile. For sensitive reports, use GitHub's private reporting features if available. Do not open a public issue containing private details.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and focused on keeping the project safe and productive.
